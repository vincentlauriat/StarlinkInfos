features

Everything your dish already knows

The dish exposes a detailed local API on its own network — StarlinkInfos just puts it on screen, refreshed every couple of seconds.

📡

Real-time dashboard

Connection status, uptime, latency, throughput, GPS lock, Ethernet speed and dish tilt — polled live from the dish itself.

📈

Latency & throughput charts

The last 15 minutes of ping and download/upload speed, straight from the dish's own history buffer.

🛰️

Obstruction map

The exact sky-view SNR grid the dish uses to detect obstructions — rendered as a live radar view.

🔁

Dish controls

Reboot or stow/unstow the dish in one click, always behind a confirmation — nothing fires by accident.

📰

Starlink news

A live feed of Starlink coverage, in French or English depending on your app language.

🚀

Launches & constellation

Upcoming Starlink launches with a countdown, plus a live count of satellites currently in orbit.

See it work

get started

Up and running in 30 seconds

Signed, notarized and Hardened-Runtime — it opens without any Gatekeeper warning. Just be on the Starlink network to see live dish data.

Download

Grab the latest .dmg from GitHub Releases and mount it.

Install

Drag StarlinkInfos.app into /Applications.

Launch

Open it while connected to your Starlink Wi-Fi. Requires macOS 15 or later.
